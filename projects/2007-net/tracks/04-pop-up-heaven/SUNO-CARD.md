## 04 — POP-UP HEAVEN

目标：160 BPM，C# minor，2:36。

### Title

```text
POP-UP HEAVEN
```

### Style

```text
English hyperpop and underground rage rap, 160 BPM, C-sharp minor. Adult male clipped lead, an exaggerated high-formant advertising alter ego made from the same voice. An original four-note synthetic jingle, hard upper synth grit, controlled mono sub and stop-start drums. Short first-verse lines, spacious two-bar phrases in the longer second verse. Catchy alternating chant and click-click responses. An eight-bar break ends in one silent bar. Final chorus begins dry and sparse, then returns to full drums. End with a normal unprocessed phone-call voice. Clear consonants and deliberate contrast.
```

### Exclude

```text
unbroken wall of noise, long guitar solo, orchestral trailer drums, constant vocal doubling, crushed sub bass, cheerful corporate pop
```
