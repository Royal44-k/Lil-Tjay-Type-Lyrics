### Title

### Style

```text
English digicore and rage rap, 156 BPM, B minor, restless and playful. Adult male first verse and adult female second verse, distinct dry tones. Male first hook, female second hook, shared final hook. Memorable chant with open ay vowels and space after each title phrase. Bright detuned upper synth, short sliding mono 808, punchy half-time drums, sharply cut original wordless vocal textures. Two-bar rap phrases with rapid internal bursts and breathing room. An eight-bar cut-up drum break leads to an alternating dialogue. Emotional friction and humor, intelligible words, compact synth layers.
```

### Exclude

```text
continuous screaming, novelty cartoon voice, washed-out consonants, festival riser, full-time choir, excessive sub distortion
```
