### Title

### Style

```text
English melodic rap and R&B over a sunny two-step groove, 132 BPM, A major. Adult female leads the hook, male first verse, female second verse, shared final refrain. Buoyant conversational two-bar rap phrases, open vowels and a few real smiles. Chopped original electric-piano attacks, elastic bass, shuffled hats, dry claps, a brief original laugh before the snare and subtle outdoor recording texture. Warm rooftop energy with clear room around the words. Keep the chorus simple and communal, let the bridge breathe, finish with a short natural spoken ending.
```

### Exclude

```text
melancholy piano ballad, festival EDM build, continuous vocal chops, glossy corporate pop, excessive vinyl crackle, dense string pad
```
