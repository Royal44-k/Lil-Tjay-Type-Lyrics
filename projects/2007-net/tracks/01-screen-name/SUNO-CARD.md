## 01 — SCREEN NAME

目标：142 BPM，F# minor，3:43。

### Title

```text
SCREEN NAME
```

### Style

```text
English melodic trap with dream R&B warmth, 142 BPM, F-sharp minor. Adult male mid-high lead, intimate grain, conversational rap verses in spacious two-bar phrases, a simple sung refrain with long vowels on slow and glow. Brief dry female question at the opening and ending. Rounded tuned 808, sparse half-time snare, glassy FM pluck, a few human guitar noises and soft room detail. Start the chorus after a short intro. Pull the bright layer out for the second verse, widen only the final chorus. Early personal-web intimacy heard through contemporary production, clear foreground vocals and an unforced ending.
```

### Exclude

```text
festival EDM drop, dense orchestral strings, nonstop ad-libs, long ambient intro, robotic lead diction, boomy reverb
```
