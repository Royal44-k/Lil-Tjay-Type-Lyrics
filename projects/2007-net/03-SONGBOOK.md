# 2007.net — Complete English Songbook

Original fictional writing. All seven complete songs; repeated choruses are printed in full.

## 01 — SCREEN NAME

```text
[Intro - female voice, close and dry]
You there?
The little light comes on.

[Chorus - male melodic lead]
Say my screen name.
Say it real slow.
I made a little window
Where the shy kids go.
Say my screen name.
Let the whole room glow.
You don't know my face yet.
There's a face I want to show.

[Verse 1 - male, conversational rap]
Twenty-two, still eating by the kitchen sink,
Blue light on the cup with the chipped-off lip.
Shift ran late; got grease in the keyboard.
Changed three letters, felt a little less ignored.
Made my page black, stars in the border,
Moved that bad photo a little bit lower.
Told you I was quiet; you sent back, "Same."
Funny how a stranger made room for my name.
I could sell a smile at the counter all day,
Get home, need an hour just to know what to say.
Typed out a joke, then I killed the last line.
You said, "Take your time." So I took my time.
No grand entrance, no borrowed car,
Just a beat I made and a cheap guitar.
You heard the loose string buzz on the take,
Said, "Leave that in." I stopped feeling fake.

[Pre-Chorus]
Somewhere past the laundry,
Somewhere past the wall,
Somebody's still awake.
I don't feel quite so small.

[Chorus]
Say my screen name.
Say it real slow.
I made a little window
Where the shy kids go.
Say my screen name.
Let the whole room glow.
You don't know my face yet.
There's a face I want to show.

[Verse 2 - male, more rhythmically open]
I sent a song with the ending unfinished,
You sent a room with the dishes still in it.
First little proof you had a life off the page:
A coat on a chair and a clock running late.
You asked what I wanted. I answered, "A break."
You asked from what. Had to sit with the weight.
Maybe the apron, maybe the rent,
Maybe the version of me I present.
Your laugh cut out where the file got small;
I filled in a laugh that was mine after all.
That's how it starts, making someone a dream.
I caught myself. Asked how your day had been.
You said, "Pretty bad." Didn't polish the truth.
We let it be bad for a minute or two.
Sun hit the spoon that I'd left by the sink.
You had to go. I had something to think.

[Bridge - half-sung]
Tomorrow, if I freeze up,
Give me a second or two.
The name I picked is easy.
The rest is coming through.

[Final Chorus]
Say my screen name.
Say it real slow.
I made a little window
Where the shy kids go.
Say my screen name.
Let the whole room glow.
You don't know my face yet.
There's a face I want to show.

[Outro - female, unprocessed]
You there?
Yeah, take your time.
```

## 02 — TOP EIGHT

```text
[Intro - male, clipped]
Hold up.
Where'd my picture go?

[Chorus - male chant with female responses]
Take me out your top eight.
Keep me in your day.
I don't need the front page.
Just a little face-to-face.

[Verse 1 - male rap]
New shoes in the thumbnail, old shoes on the bus,
Moved a face above mine; made a court case of us.
Refresh, get dressed, act like I ain't checked.
Run into you downstairs, won't make eye contact.
I put the loudest people nearest to my name,
Like a little rented crowd could keep me entertained.
Left Ben out the photo, said the crop looked clean.
He was holding all our jackets just outside the scene.
Then I cracked a joke about the soles on his shoes.
Whole room laughed. I had finally made the news.
Same black pair, same floor under my feet;
I hid my own worn heel underneath the seat.
You moved one square. I declared a whole war.
Didn't ask what you'd been dealing with before.
I can count the faces; can't account for that.
Your name on my phone. I should call you back.

[Chorus - female takes the lead]
Take me out your top eight.
Keep me in your day.
I don't need the front page.
Just a little face-to-face.

[Verse 2 - female, dry and direct]
I was at the laundromat, quarters in my coat,
You were reading meanings into everything I wrote.
Moved my sister higher; she was having a week.
Didn't think a little square would mean we couldn't speak.
You want proof? There's a key beside the mat.
You left soup in my fridge; I remember that.
Keep a hundred pictures if you like how they look.
I won't take attendance in your digital book.
Still, I did the same thing. Let me get that straight:
Saw you with some new friends, showed up half an hour late.
Wanted you to notice how I wasn't in the shot,
Spent the whole night distant just to say that we were not.
We got good at making everybody guess.
I can say I'm hurt without inventing a test.
Put the jacket on. Meet me by the gate.
Bring your jacket down. It's getting late.

[Break - wordless chopped voices]

[Bridge - male and female alternate]
I kept score.
So did I.
Made a scene.
Missed the ride.
Still got time?
For the walk.
No big speech.
We can talk.

[Final Chorus - shared]
Take me out your top eight.
Keep me in your day.
I don't need the front page.
Just a little face-to-face.
Take me out your top eight.
Keep me in your day.
Leave the little boxes.
I'll be waiting by the gate.

[Outro - male]
Got the jacket.
Coming down.
```

## 03 — LOW-RES KISS

```text
[Intro - female, smiling]
Wait, don't delete that one.
That's my favorite.

[Verse 1 - female singing rap]
Last bus, lip gloss on the paper cup,
You make a serious face; I mess it up.
Flash hits the window, puts a sun on your ear.
I laugh so hard that my eyes disappear.
You got half a haircut hiding under your cap,
I got one cheap earring that keeps falling back.
Try to hold still; then the driver turns.
Catch my waist where the cold ring burns.
No perfect angle, no useful pose,
Just your sleeve where I wipe my nose.
You ask if I'm warm; I move your hand.
There, by my shoulder. You understand.

[Pre-Chorus - female]
All that light gets lost.
Somehow, this gets through.

[Chorus - female lead, male low harmony]
Low-res kiss.
Keep it like this.
Half of your smile
And none of the distance.
Leave it blurry.
I know it's us.
Nothing in focus,
But I know enough.

[Verse 2 - male melodic rap]
You kept the one I tried to take again.
Said my good side changes when I'm with a friend.
I learned your face from more than where it shines:
Sleep on the left cheek, little pillow lines.
At your door, you search for the proper key.
I could stand here longer than I need to be.
You look up. I ask. You pull me near.
Somebody's television talking through the stairs.
Later, when the little file won't load,
I'll still know the corner where we missed our road.
Can't get that warmth from a sharper view.
The hand on my shoulder was attached to you.

[Bridge - duet, intimate]
Keep the bad flash.
Keep the bus light.
I was laughing.
You were right.

[Chorus - duet]
Low-res kiss.
Keep it like this.
Half of your smile
And none of the distance.
```

## 04 — POP-UP HEAVEN

```text
[Intro - pitched version of the lead voice]
Your new life is waiting.
Right this way.

[Verse 1 - male, clipped rap]
Won a million from a flashing square,
Still had three dollars for the fare.
Free trial face with a full-price jaw,
Never seen a man so completely without flaws.
Downloaded confidence; came with a bar
Across my whole screen saying who we are.
They knew I liked blue. They knew I liked bass.
Sold me a room with my name on the place.
I made a beat on a borrowed machine,
Watched it get buried by a brighter green.
Maybe put a chain in the picture? Gold.
Maybe act bored? Maybe act cold?
Friend said, "That's good." I said, "Good isn't big."
Spent my whole pay on a camera rig.
Filmed an empty chair like somebody sat there.
Left it in the shot. Called it atmosphere.

[Chorus - tight melodic chant]
Pop-up heaven.
Let me in.
One more window.
Same old skin.
Click, click, close it.
Back again.
Pop-up heaven.
Where's the exit?

[Verse 2 - male, accelerating]
I could make the hunger look expensive in a frame,
Crop the kitchen counter, put a letter by my name.
Every little number got a job inside my chest:
One to make me nervous, one to keep me from my rest.
I got sick of hearing I should simply want it less.
Easy when your room comes with a view and an address.
I want money. I want options. I want Mom off double shifts.
Want a song somebody plays without me begging them to click.
But I made her late-night call a background to the plan.
Kept adjusting lighting while she asked me how I am.
"Busy." She said, "Eating?" I said, "Yeah," and moved the plate.
There's a cold unbroken sandwich just outside the frame.
Little spinning circle, keep me company again.
When the screen goes black, I can see the room I'm in.
Can't select a better face. Can't close my own eyes.
Let the fan keep turning. Let me hear the outside.

[Break - instrumental, abrupt silence on the last bar]

[Bridge - low, nearly spoken]
Bus outside.
Someone laughing.
Tap downstairs.
Nothing flashing.
Take one breath.
Make one call.
Let it ring.
Leave it all.

[Final Chorus - first half dry, second half full]
Pop-up heaven.
Let me in.
One more window.
Same old skin.
Click, click, close it.
Back again.
Pop-up heaven.
Where's the exit?

[Outro - unprocessed]
Hi, Mom.
Yeah, I'm eating now.
```

## 05 — DRAFTS AT 3:17

```text
[Intro - male, close]
Dear Ben.
No. Just Ben.

[Verse 1 - male, measured rap]
Three seventeen, and the kettle clicks off.
I write, "It's been a minute." Like a minute got lost.
Like a clock made the joke, like the room made me laugh,
Like I didn't see you pulling both your feet back.
You lent me bus fare when my card wouldn't read.
I made your shoes a story everybody could repeat.
Same thin soles I was trying to hide;
Found a softer place to put the thing I felt inside.
That's a reason. Doesn't make it yours to bear.
I erase the little paragraph explaining why I cared.
Erase the weather, erase the show,
Erase the "if" from "if I made you feel low."
I keep wanting one sentence that'll settle the debt,
Something you can answer with "It's cool, forget."
But you walked home quiet. I remember the sound:
Everybody talking while you stared at the ground.

[Chorus - restrained low melody]
Three seventeen.
Backspace, breathe.
All these words
And none of me.
Three seventeen.
Say what you mean.
Sorry looks small
On a crowded screen.

[Verse 2 - male, pauses between thoughts]
There's a picture on my desk from before that night.
You were holding up the lamp so the rest of us looked right.
Never in the middle, always helping something work;
I confused the way you loved us with permission to be hurt.
I could send a song. You used to like the chords.
But a chorus can't do every little errand I avoid.
So: I was cruel. You had done nothing wrong.
I let them think I meant it. I let it go on.
I won't make a doorway out of what I did to you,
Ask you to congratulate the man I'm turning into.
If you want the distance, I can let the distance stand.
If you want the jacket, I can leave it with your dad.
Cursor by the button. Thumb against a tooth.
Funny, all this typing, still afraid to tell the truth.
I save another draft. The morning takes the room.
For once, I don't mistake the words for following through.

[Bridge - spare spoken melody]
Three seventeen became four.
Four became the bus.
I carried what I'd written.
I hadn't carried much.
Your jacket by the doorway,
My hand upon the sleeve.
I know what I should do now.
Knowing isn't doing it.

[Chorus - no ad-libs]
Three seventeen.
Backspace, breathe.
All these words
And none of me.
Three seventeen.
Say what you mean.
Sorry looks small
On a crowded screen.

[Outro - sampled female question, then space]
You there?
```

## 06 — BURN THIS SUMMER

```text
[Intro - female, spontaneous]
Is it recording?
Don't stop it.

[Chorus - female lead, male answer]
Burn this summer.
Track by track.
If the disc starts skipping,
Let it bring you back.
Windows open.
Sing it bad.
We don't need forever,
Just the day we had.

[Verse 1 - male, buoyant melodic rap]
Blue marker, silver disc, crooked little sun,
Named the folder FINAL; made another one.
Got your laugh before the snare, left it in the mix,
You said, "I sound ridiculous." I said, "That's the bit."
Roof too hot, so we sit on folded tees,
Half a bag of melting ice between our knees.
No show booked, no backstage pass,
Just a mic through the window and a car going past.
You are leaving for a job where they spell your name wrong.
Said you'll teach them on Monday; put your favorite take on.
We make plans like a plan can make the distance behave,
Then you ask for another verse instead of being brave.
I send Ben one message: "I was out of line."
Leave it there, don't decorate, don't send it seven times.
You turn the speaker toward me, wait until I sing.
The whole thing clips. We keep the evening in.

[Post-Chorus - female and male]
Track by track.
Bring it back.
Leave that laugh.
Leave that laugh.

[Verse 2 - female, playful then direct]
I take the pen and draw a face beside the sun,
Write the names of all the people making up this one.
You keep saying, "When you're famous, you can use that take."
I say, "Can I want the job without you calling it a break?"
I like making songs. I like getting paid on time.
I can have a life that doesn't fit inside your rhyme.
Still, when we hit the chorus, I go louder than I should.
Someone from a window shouts; I can't tell if it's good.
Warm soda, long shadows, permanent pen,
Things we never thought to call a memory back then.
Make another copy; give the good one to your mom.
Leave the little count-in where I couldn't find the one.
No promise every person will be here next June.
No need to spoil the evening just to prove we know it too.
I lean into the microphone before the light is gone.
You play that crooked first chord. I find a note that's wrong.

[Chorus - shared]
Burn this summer.
Track by track.
If the disc starts skipping,
Let it bring you back.
Windows open.
Sing it bad.
We don't need forever,
Just the day we had.

[Bridge - shared, open space]
Someone drops a bottle cap.
Someone misses their cue.
We leave the road noise running.
It belongs in here too.

[Final Chorus - shared]
Burn this summer.
Track by track.
If the disc starts skipping,
Let it bring you back.
We don't need forever,
Just the day we had.

[Outro - female]
Label side up.
Don't lose it.
```

## 07 — 2007.NET

```text
[Intro - male, present day]
Two thousand seven.
Dot net.

[Verse 1 - male, reflective rap]
Found the drive in the box with the keys,
Under Christmas lights and some blank CDs.
My old name still has numbers at the end.
I forgot the password; I remember them.
There's that room I swore I'd never miss,
There's a younger face with a practiced lip.
Open up the photo; let the loading finish.
Everyone is here. Everyone is different.
Ben's got a daughter with his serious eyes.
We trade birthday messages; don't rewrite the night.
Mara's got a shop and a laugh I still know.
She says, "Keep that picture. Please don't post it, though."
I type, "Of course." And I leave it where it lives.
You can love a memory without making it a gift
To a room of total strangers, asking them to see
How happy all these people used to look with me.

[Chorus - male lead, female harmony]
I was here.
I was here.
You can close the window.
I won't disappear.
Keep the song.
Let it end.
I can miss that summer
Without moving back again.

[Verse 2 - male, natural speech rhythm]
There's a draft I never sent, another that I did.
There's a joke I wish I'd swallowed, all the little ways I hid.
I won't call it innocence because the edges blur.
Some of us were laughing. Some of us got hurt.
Still, the beat comes in; I know the next four bars.
My hand begins to tap before I get that far.
Twenty-two was lonely, reckless, sometimes kind.
I can hold the whole year without making it a shrine.
Disc skips at the bridge. I used to hate that part.
Now I hear the room breathe just before we start.
There's her laugh, there's the road, there's a note I couldn't reach.
I sing it lower now. It sits a little better with me.
Daylight on the desk. Got a call I said I'd make.
Close the folder gently. Put a kettle on to wait.
I don't need the old name to pick up when it rings.
That little opening chord can come along with me.

[Bridge - female archive and present male alternate]
You there?
I'm here.
You there?
I'm here.
Same small question.
Different room.
Let the last note finish.
I've got things to do.

[Final Chorus - lead and harmony]
I was here.
I was here.
You can close the window.
I won't disappear.
Keep the song.
Let it end.
I can miss that summer
Without moving back again.
I was here.
I'm still here.

[Outro - male, dry]
I'm here.
How've you been?
```
