# SL01 — NO RECEIPT

原版。154 BPM / F# minor；男中音；96 小节。第一段面对合约包装，第二段反问自己如何对待合作者。不要误用 Split Sheet Remix 的三人接力歌词。

### Title

```text
NO RECEIPT
```

### Style

```text
English-language rage rap with an original punk-guitar sample flip and half-time trap drums. 154 BPM, F-sharp minor. Dry, grounded male midrange rap; a clipped six-syllable title chant, minimal melisma, short group answers. Jagged resampled guitar, wide detuned synth stabs, heavy mono 808, dry snare, sparse hats. Use an original female vocal fragment, "still," as a percussive recurring stab. Four-bar filtered intro; immediate hook; two story-driven verses; voice-and-kick breakdown; a full-beat silence before the last hook. Each verse line spans two fast bars. Leave space after punchlines. Final hook adds one higher counterline rather than more distortion. Defiant but thoughtful: value, authorship, paying collaborators. Strong groove and intelligible consonants; controlled low end, upfront vocal, width above the bass, real pauses and dynamic contrast.
```

### Exclude

```text
EDM festival drops, pop-punk lead singing, metal screaming, constant double-time rapping, nonstop hi-hat rolls, muddy sub-bass, excessive reverb on lead vocals, long instrumental intro, imitation of a named artist
```

### 人声方向｜用于候选修正或录音指导

### Vocal direction

```text
Use a grounded adult male midrange with a dry grain, clear American English consonants and measured rhythmic speech. Give the chorus a clipped melodic chant, not a scream. Double only the title and selected end words. Verse two should become more reflective before it grows firmer. Any female still fragment belongs in gaps and must never cover the lead.
```

### 纯伴奏备选｜使用器乐模式，歌词留空

### Instrumental Style

```text
Instrumental rage-trap, 154 BPM, F-sharp minor. Short resampled punk-guitar fragments, sparse detuned synth stabs, heavy centered 808 and dry half-time snare. Four-bar filtered intro, eight-bar hook, two twenty-four-bar verse spaces. Keep the first verse narrow. Remove kick for the first eight bars of verse two. A voice-space breakdown becomes sparse kick and guitar, with the last beat empty before the final hook. End sharply. Leave the lead-vocal frequency and rhythm space open.
```

### Instrumental Exclude

```text
lead vocals, backing vocals, choir, spoken words, sung lyrics
```

### Hook 不清楚时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Hook repair

```text
Keep the exact chorus words and the six-syllable No receipt, no return cell. Stress receipt on its second syllable. Make return, learn, burn and turn clear landing words. Leave a gap after each phrase and use short backing answers rather than a continuous crowd layer.
```

### 编排偏离时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Arrangement repair

```text
Keep the trap pocket central. Cut the guitar into short rhythmic fragments rather than strumming continuously. Narrow the verses and remove the kick in the opening eight bars of verse two. Leave the last beat of the breakdown empty. Add one high counterline for the final hook without raising distortion.
```
