# SLP07 — LAST TRAIN, FIRST LIGHT

LAST TRAIN, FIRST LIGHT。156 BPM / E minor；100 小节；男高音。源钢琴与提琴按 78 BPM 四小节写作，在目标网格占八小节。

### Title

```text
LAST TRAIN, FIRST LIGHT
```

### Style

```text
Expressive male tenor, clear melodic rap and an open memorable hook. English piano trap, 156 BPM, E minor with relative-major warmth. An original piano ostinato sampled from a slower performance, lyrical solo violin answering only between vocal phrases, rounded 808, half-time snare and restrained hats. Story: leaving home with affection, arriving somewhere imperfect and beginning a new life. Two fast bars per verse line. Keep family details conversational. Expose piano for the bridge, then widen the final chorus without changing tempo. Bittersweet momentum, breath and forward motion, clean lead above the strings.
```

### Exclude

```text
cinematic trailer drums, grandiose choir, busy violin under verses, generic motivational speech
```

Leave room for the violin answers.
