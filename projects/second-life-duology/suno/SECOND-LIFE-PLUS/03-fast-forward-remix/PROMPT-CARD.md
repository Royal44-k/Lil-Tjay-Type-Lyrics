# SLP03 — FAST FORWARD (Night Shift Remix)

Night Shift Remix。138 BPM / D minor；92 小节。女声掌握 Hook；男 Verse 16 小节，女 Verse 24 小节；Bridge 男/女/男/女。

### Title

```text
FAST FORWARD (Night Shift Remix)
```

### Style

```text
Female alto owns the melodic hook; restrained male tenor sings the first verse, female sings the longer reply, then a brief duet. English piano-led melodic trap and R&B, 138 BPM, D minor, half-time pocket. Keep the repeated Fast-forward motif and three descending wait notes. Warm chopped upright piano, rounded 808, soft violin answers, sparse trap drums. Two contrasting lives: a traveling musician and the partner working nights. Replace glitch excitement with sustained emotional tension. Two fast bars per verse line. Final chorus adds reciprocal male harmony; keep the lead dry, close and human.
```

### Exclude

```text
hyperpop glitches, cartoon vocal pitching, EDM drops, constant ad-libs
```

### 人声方向｜用于候选修正或录音指导

### Vocal direction

```text
Put a grounded adult female alto in charge of the introduction, every main hook and the longer second verse. Use a restrained male tenor for verse one and the pre-chorus. The bridge alternates male, female, male, female one line each. In the final chorus, the male supports rather than replaces her lead. Preserve her pride in her own work and make the disagreement sound adult and specific.
```

### 纯伴奏备选｜使用器乐模式，歌词留空

### Instrumental Style

```text
Instrumental piano melodic trap and R&B, 138 BPM, D minor. Reordered short piano phrases from a slower original performance, rounded 808, dry half-time drums and few decorative sounds. Early eight-bar hook, sixteen-bar first verse, four-bar pre-chorus, another hook, twenty-four-bar second verse. Eight-bar conversational bridge and sixteen-bar final hook.
```
