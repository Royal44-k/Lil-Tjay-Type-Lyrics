# SLP05 — ROOM TONE (Open Door Remix)

Open Door Remix。124 BPM / A major；80 小节。男 Verse 1、女 Verse 2；Hook 二重唱；Bridge 女/男/女/男，Outro 女声。

### Title

```text
ROOM TONE (Open Door Remix)
```

### Style

```text
Intimate male and female singing-rap duet. Male first verse, female second verse, shared hook, four short alternating bridge lines. English alternative R&B with a light two-step UK garage rhythm, 124 BPM, A major. Keep the original descending quiet, quiet phrase. Chopped electric piano, soft sub, shuffled hats, dry rimshot and small breath chops between phrases. The other partner finally speaks; tender but unresolved, clearer boundaries and mutual listening. Two bars per verse line. Keep the room small and the voices separate, without theatrical belting or a huge final build.
```

### Exclude

```text
power-ballad climax, stadium choir, aggressive rage synths, heavy vocal effects
```

### 人声方向｜用于候选修正或录音指导

### Vocal direction

```text
Use a small adult male-female duet. The male first verse must finish naturally before the female second verse replies. Keep the hook shared and intimate. The bridge alternates female, male, female, male, one line each; the female takes the final Your turn, Im listening. Sing conversationally even when the drums dance. Keep disagreement audible without turning it into a shouting contest.
```

### 纯伴奏备选｜使用器乐模式，歌词留空

### Instrumental Style

```text
Instrumental alternative R&B and light two-step UK garage, 124 BPM, A major. Short electric-piano chord slices, rounded bass, syncopated kick, dry rim and gently late hats. Four-bar intro, eight-bar hook and two sixteen-bar verse spaces separated by the hook. Change keyboard inversions for the second verse, remove bass during the eight-bar bridge, then a sixteen-bar intimate final chorus. Keep the scale warm and human rather than festival-sized.
```

### Instrumental Exclude

```text
lead vocals, backing vocals, choir, spoken words, sung lyrics
```

### Hook 不清楚时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Hook repair

```text
Preserve the original seven-note Stay for the quiet, quiet identity in the new major-key harmony. Keep the remix lines about finishing a sentence and leaving distance. Share the chorus without smearing consonants. Preserve the final line about coming back another day. Do not restore the original R&B chorus.
```

### 编排偏离时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Arrangement repair

```text
Keep the two-step kick pattern rather than four-on-the-floor. Let chord slices answer the drums and avoid filling every vocal gap. Change inversions for the female reply without simply raising its volume. Remove bass in the bridge and let each speaker finish before the other enters.
```
