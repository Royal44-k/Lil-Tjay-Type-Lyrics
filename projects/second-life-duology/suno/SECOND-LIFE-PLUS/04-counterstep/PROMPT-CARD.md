# SLP04 — COUNTERSTEP

COUNTERSTEP。144 BPM / G minor；96 小节；灵活男声。V02 原演奏为 108 BPM 三拍，目标为 144 BPM 四拍；这是编排目标，文本不能强制采样精确对齐。

### Title

```text
COUNTERSTEP
```

### Style

```text
Playful male rap, sharp consonants and a compact singable chant. English orchestral drill, 144 BPM, G minor. Build the beat around an original short violin waltz phrase: retain its lilting three-part motion over a firm four-beat drill grid. Dry spiccato bow attacks, a few pizzicato replies, sparse 808 glides and syncopated kick. The hook is Wrong foot, right time; confident and welcoming, not menacing. Verses tell a dance-floor story of turning a stumble into a move. Brief exposed violin bridge, then a punchy final hook. Two fast bars per verse line; leave room around punchlines.
```

### Exclude

```text
cinematic trailer percussion, long legato string pads, violent sound effects, EDM risers
```
