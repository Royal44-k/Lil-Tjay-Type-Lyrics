# SLP06 — SOFT LANDING

SOFT LANDING。76 BPM / Eb major；52 小节；低女声。两小节 Intro 即入 Hook；主歌一行一小节，Hook 一行两小节。

### Title

```text
SOFT LANDING
```

### Style

```text
Warm low female lead, natural adult voice, intimate singing rap and a memorable six-note refrain. English R&B and trap-soul, 76 BPM, E-flat major. Original felt-piano arpeggios cut into a soft loop, sparse pizzicato violin replies, rounded bass, light rimshot and quiet hats. Affectionate, sensual and playful: laughter, asking clearly, unhurried closeness. Verses stay conversational at one line per bar; the hook opens into longer notes. Small harmonies only on phrase endings. A warm room with clear words and low-volume detail; let the final breath finish naturally.
```

### Exclude

```text
gospel belting, heavy 808 distortion, long vocal runs, big orchestral swells
```

### 人声方向｜用于候选修正或录音指导

### Vocal direction

```text
Use a warm low adult female voice with clear diction, relaxed singing rap and occasional natural smiles. Keep the short title phrase simple. Let questions and replies feel spoken at close range. Hold time and mine in the chorus, but keep verse function words light. Sensuality comes from timing and distance, not continuous breath effects or long runs.
```

### 纯伴奏备选｜使用器乐模式，歌词留空

### Instrumental Style

```text
Instrumental intimate R&B and trap-soul, 76 BPM, E-flat major. Soft felt-piano arpeggio slices, rounded bass, gentle dry snare and a few high pizzicato-violin answers. Two-bar intro, immediate eight-bar hook, short eight-bar verse spaces and a four-bar pre-chorus. Keep a subtle pulse during the stripped bridge. Add only a small extra harmony color in the final hook and preserve the two-bar release. Warm, tactile and unhurried.
```

### Instrumental Exclude

```text
lead vocals, backing vocals, choir, spoken words, sung lyrics
```

### Hook 不清楚时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Hook repair

```text
Keep Soft landing simple and recognizable. Stress land more than ing. Let time and mine carry the first pair of lines, near and here the second. Preserve Tell me what you want with clear words and comfortable space. Remove ornamental runs that delay the next line.
```

### 编排偏离时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Arrangement repair

```text
Keep piano as the main texture. Use pizzicato answers only in actual vocal gaps and remove them when the lead sustains. Reduce bass weight if it obscures low female diction. Keep the final hook intimate and retain a complete natural ending.
```
