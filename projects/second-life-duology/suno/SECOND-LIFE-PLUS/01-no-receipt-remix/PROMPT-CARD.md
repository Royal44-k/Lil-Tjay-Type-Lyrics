# SLP01 — NO RECEIPT (Split Sheet Remix)

Split Sheet Remix。140 BPM / F# minor；96 小节。Verse 1 艺人男中音、Verse 2 制作人低男声、Verse 3 女工程师。保留六音首句，其余叙事重写。

### Title

```text
NO RECEIPT (Split Sheet Remix)
```

### Style

```text
Three distinct adult rap voices trading verses: measured male midrange, clipped low male, precise female alto. English drill and underground hip-hop, 140 BPM, F-sharp minor. Preserve the compact title chant; change the perspective to collaborators confronting unequal credit. Dry chopped piano, short pizzicato violin answers, syncopated kick, sparse sliding 808, tight snare. Keep each verse voice separate and intelligible, two fast bars per lyric line. Strip the bridge to piano and voice; final hook becomes a shared answer. Tense, clear and controlled, with real spaces between phrases.
```

### Exclude

```text
EDM build-ups, continuous orchestral pads, metal screaming, chipmunk vocals
```

### 人声方向｜用于候选修正或录音指导

### Vocal direction

```text
Give the three verses clearly different adult personalities: a measured midrange male artist, a lower clipped male producer, and a precise female alto engineer. Each verse must finish before the next begins. Let the producer and engineer lead the early shared refrain; the final refrain can gather all three, with the artist owning the last I will say we commitment. Keep words clear and ad-libs brief.
```

### 纯伴奏备选｜使用器乐模式，歌词留空

### Instrumental Style

```text
Instrumental drill and underground hip-hop, 140 BPM, F-sharp minor. Chopped dry piano attacks, short pizzicato violin replies, tuned mono 808, sparse sharp snare and restrained hats. Four-bar intro and eight-bar hook. Make three sixteen-bar verse spaces: narrow first, a few bass slides in the second, hats reduced at the start of the third. Eight-bar stripped bridge, then a sixteen-bar final hook. Leave the final bridge beat silent. Tense, controlled and spacious.
```

### Instrumental Exclude

```text
lead vocals, backing vocals, choir, spoken words, sung lyrics
```

### Hook 不清楚时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Hook repair

```text
Preserve the original six-note No receipt, no return cell and the new remix chorus words. Make names and work carry the second line. Keep the contrast between we and I clear. The last I will say we line belongs to the artist, not to an anonymous crowd. Do not replace the remix with the original chorus.
```

### 编排偏离时｜已有候选的局部修正
