# SLP02 — IVORY HOURS

IVORY HOURS。88 BPM / D minor；60 小节；低男声。主歌一行一小节。钢琴与磁带属于虚构叙事，不能假称已录父亲声音。

### Title

```text
IVORY HOURS
```

### Style

```text
Low male voice, close conversational rap with a small restrained sung refrain. English underground hip-hop and jazz rap, 88 BPM, D minor. An original upright-piano performance resampled into uneven two-beat fragments; audible felt and damper detail, warm bass, dry kick and rimshot, light swing. Reflective family storytelling: grief and affection coexist with anger. Verses use rhythmic speech, one line per bar; chorus uses longer notes and breathing space. Expose the piano in the bridge. Honest and intimate, clear consonants, natural pauses, no grand emotional climax.
```

### Exclude

```text
power-ballad belting, dense hi-hat rolls, stadium choir, heavy vinyl noise
```

### Lyrics

```text
[Intro]

[Chorus]
Ivory hours, nobody claps.
I let the last note find its way back.
You never said sorry; I still hear you laugh.
Both things can fit in the song that I have.

[Verse 1 - close, conversational]
Back from the service, I sit at your keys,
Cup-ring moon on the lid by the lamp.
I could sell it for less than the movers would need,
But I can't lift the phone with that number in hand.
You taught me the left hand and hated my timing,
Tapped on the wood till I swallowed the tune.
I learned to play softly to keep you from sighing;
Now I hit that wrong note, let it stay in the room.
Mom says you mellowed. My brother says hardly.
We carry a different old man in our heads.
He fixed every hinge, never said he was sorry;
I'm sorting the screws from the things left unsaid.

[Chorus]
Ivory hours, nobody claps.
I let the last note find its way back.
You never said sorry; I still hear you laugh.
Both things can fit in the song that I have.

[Verse 2 - low, reflective]
Found a small tape with a date on the label,
You counting me in, then forgetting the count.
I laugh at the part where you knock on the table;
You laugh right behind me. I hadn't heard that sound.
For years I had filed you in one little folder:
The voice in the hall when the bottle got low.
But this doesn't cancel the nights I got older
Before I was ready, or make them less so.
I keep both the takes when I cut up the sample,
The soft little hum and the crack in the chair.
Forgiveness is still not a word I can manage;
Tonight I just leave a small gap in the snare.

[Bridge - almost spoken]
I don't have to make you good
To miss you when the house gets still.

[Final Chorus]
Ivory hours, nobody claps.
I let the last note find its way back.
You never said sorry; I still hear you laugh.
I leave a little air for the parts I don't have.

[Outro]
One note hanging.
I let it hang.
```

### 人声方向｜用于候选修正或录音指导

### Vocal direction

```text
Use an adult low male voice, close and speech-led, with restrained melodic endings. Hold tenderness and resentment together without exaggerated crying. Give I-vo-ry three sung syllables with the first strongest, and sing hours as one syllable in the hook. Keep dense verse lines rhythmic and leave the two-line bridge room to breathe.
```

### 纯伴奏备选｜使用器乐模式，歌词留空

### Instrumental Style

```text
Instrumental piano-sample underground hip-hop with jazz-rap space, 88 BPM, D minor. Original upright-piano motif, sparse warm bass, dry drums and subtle uneven human timing. Present four bars of piano before the hook. Chop shorter fragments under twelve-bar verses; restore longer pieces in the hook. A small almost-empty bridge and complete piano decay at the end. The groove stays physical, the emotion stays unresolved.
```

### Instrumental Exclude

```text
lead vocals, backing vocals, choir, spoken words, sung lyrics
```
