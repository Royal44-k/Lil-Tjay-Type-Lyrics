# SECOND LIFE — 完整 Suno 制作提示词

每首单独生成。第一轮只复制 Title / Style / Exclude / Lyrics 四栏到相应位置。其他段落是人声、纯伴奏或已有候选的修正提示，一次选一个问题处理，不全部叠加进 Style。中文说明不进入歌词框。

歌词和主 Style / Exclude 为既有版本原样收录。附加方向是本轮新写。所有 BPM、调性、声部和段落数都是制作目标，未假定文本可精确控制音频。操作流程见 07-SUNO-WORKFLOW.md。

## SL01 — NO RECEIPT

原版。154 BPM / F# minor；男中音；96 小节。第一段面对合约包装，第二段反问自己如何对待合作者。不要误用 Split Sheet Remix 的三人接力歌词。

### Title

```text
NO RECEIPT
```

### Style

```text
English-language rage rap with an original punk-guitar sample flip and half-time trap drums. 154 BPM, F-sharp minor. Dry, grounded male midrange rap; a clipped six-syllable title chant, minimal melisma, short group answers. Jagged resampled guitar, wide detuned synth stabs, heavy mono 808, dry snare, sparse hats. Use an original female vocal fragment, "still," as a percussive recurring stab. Four-bar filtered intro; immediate hook; two story-driven verses; voice-and-kick breakdown; a full-beat silence before the last hook. Each verse line spans two fast bars. Leave space after punchlines. Final hook adds one higher counterline rather than more distortion. Defiant but thoughtful: value, authorship, paying collaborators. Strong groove and intelligible consonants; controlled low end, upfront vocal, width above the bass, real pauses and dynamic contrast.
```

### Exclude

```text
EDM festival drops, pop-punk lead singing, metal screaming, constant double-time rapping, nonstop hi-hat rolls, muddy sub-bass, excessive reverb on lead vocals, long instrumental intro, imitation of a named artist
```

### 人声方向｜用于候选修正或录音指导

### Vocal direction

```text
Use a grounded adult male midrange with a dry grain, clear American English consonants and measured rhythmic speech. Give the chorus a clipped melodic chant, not a scream. Double only the title and selected end words. Verse two should become more reflective before it grows firmer. Any female still fragment belongs in gaps and must never cover the lead.
```

### 纯伴奏备选｜使用器乐模式，歌词留空

### Instrumental Style

```text
Instrumental rage-trap, 154 BPM, F-sharp minor. Short resampled punk-guitar fragments, sparse detuned synth stabs, heavy centered 808 and dry half-time snare. Four-bar filtered intro, eight-bar hook, two twenty-four-bar verse spaces. Keep the first verse narrow. Remove kick for the first eight bars of verse two. A voice-space breakdown becomes sparse kick and guitar, with the last beat empty before the final hook. End sharply. Leave the lead-vocal frequency and rhythm space open.
```

### Instrumental Exclude

```text
lead vocals, backing vocals, choir, spoken words, sung lyrics
```

### Hook 不清楚时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Hook repair

```text
Keep the exact chorus words and the six-syllable No receipt, no return cell. Stress receipt on its second syllable. Make return, learn, burn and turn clear landing words. Leave a gap after each phrase and use short backing answers rather than a continuous crowd layer.
```

### 编排偏离时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Arrangement repair

```text
Keep the trap pocket central. Cut the guitar into short rhythmic fragments rather than strumming continuously. Narrow the verses and remove the kick in the opening eight bars of verse two. Leave the last beat of the breakdown empty. Add one high counterline for the final hook without raising distortion.
```

## SL02 — FAST FORWARD

原版。146 BPM / B minor；男高音；100 小节。写过劳与错过家人，保留明亮外壳下的焦虑；不是 Night Shift Remix 的男女职业对话。

### Title

```text
FAST FORWARD
```

### Style

```text
English melodic rap, hyperpop textures and half-time trap. 146 BPM, B minor. Agile male tenor, bright rising phrases with audible breaths, casual American English diction. The earworm is "Fast-forward, fast-forward, wait, wait, wait": repeat the first melodic cell, then descend on three separated "wait" notes. Glassy plucks, clipped bright chords, elastic 808, tiny drum fills. An original high female vocal chop acts as a playful reply. One full beat of silence after the last "wait" in hook lines one and three. Verses tell a concrete story of overwork and missed family time; keep them dry and understandable. Each verse line spans two fast bars. In the bridge, remove the kick and leave empty downbeats. Final chorus opens up and sheds most glitches. Euphoric surface with anxious subtext, never childish; rhythmic surprises at section boundaries, not constant effects.
```

### Exclude

```text
happy novelty song, cartoon lead voice, nonstop glitch effects, chipmunk lead vocal, EDM risers, four-on-the-floor drop, metal guitars, muddy 808, overly dense ad-libs, imitation of a named artist
```

### 人声方向｜用于候选修正或录音指导

```text
Use an agile adult male tenor, close and articulate, with audible breaths and a natural speaking center. The verses should sound restless but understandable. Reserve longer notes for the chorus. Use high female vocal fragments only as brief answers. In the final chorus, let the male delivery become less rushed and more deliberate.
```

### Instrumental Style

```text
Instrumental melodic trap with restrained hyperpop textures, 146 BPM, B minor. Glassy plucks, short bright chords, elastic 808, dry half-time snare and tiny boundary fills. Design an eight-bar hook with one full beat empty at the ends of its first and third two-bar phrases. Use two twenty-four-bar verse spaces separated by a four-bar lift and chorus. Thin the drums before the hook, remove kick in the bridge, and simplify the final chorus. Bright but tense, never cartoonish.
```


### Hook 不清楚时｜已有候选的局部修正

### Hook repair

```text
Preserve all chorus words. Repeat the same three-note Fast-forward cell twice, then separate three descending wait notes. Leave one full beat of silence at the end of chorus lines one and three. Reduce surrounding glitches until the title can be followed after one listen.
```

### 编排偏离时｜已有候选的局部修正


## SL03 — ROOM TONE

原版。92 BPM / F# minor；低女声；64 小节。主歌每行一小节，副歌每行两小节。亲密、疲惫、边界；尾奏揭示完整女声母句。

### Title

```text
ROOM TONE
```

### Style

```text
Intimate English alternative R&B and conversational singing rap. 92 BPM, F-sharp minor moving through B minor 9, E13 and A major 9. Warm low female lead, close microphone, clear natural diction, a little grain on sustained notes, no theatrical belting. "Stay for the quiet, quiet" is the melodic center: a small rise followed by a soft repeated descent. Felt electric piano, restrained guitar harmonics, rounded sub, dry rimshot, gently delayed hats and barely audible room ambience. One verse line per bar; sing-rap long lines and leave a breath before the next thought. Lyrical detail: wet jacket, chipped bowl, doing dishes, learning to ask clearly. Remove drums for the bridge. Final chorus has a new lyric about closeness without losing yourself; outro reveals the original female phrase "There's still life in me." Small, warm and tactile, spacious stereo details around a centered voice; preserve silence.
```

### Exclude

```text
power-ballad vocals, gospel belting, big orchestral climax, aggressive rage synths, constant vocal runs, lo-fi mud, vinyl crackle covering words, long ambient intro, excessively wet lead vocal, imitation of a named artist
```

## SL04 — SECOND LIFE

两张专辑共用的原曲。144 BPM / A major；96 小节；男主唱与女声回答。六个原资产保持一致。有已选定原音频时沿用该录音，不为 PLUS 再生成一个新 take。

### Title

```text
SECOND LIFE
```

### Style

```text
English melodic rap and soulful sample-based trap, with a short self-played breakbeat passage and chamber strings. 144 BPM, A major, warm half-time pocket. Grounded male lead, easy melodic title hook, distinct warm female answers. Open with an original female phrase, "There's still life in me"; let that voice answer the male hook. Scuffed piano sample, rounded 808, dry claps, a small human-sounding drum break, cello pulse and high string replies. Narrative verses about repairing a speaker, losing a neighborhood shop and building a shared music room. Each verse line spans two fast bars. Bridge opens into breakbeat motion without changing tempo. Final chorus brings earlier vocal fragments together, leaves room for the words and feels communal rather than grandiose. Last four bars expose the female source phrase and its breath. Hope with weight, tactile detail, strong bass clarity, no triumphalist wall of sound.
```

### Exclude

```text
cinematic trailer drums, sentimental power ballad, stadium rock, children's choir, relentless brass, excessive gospel runs, generic motivational spoken monologue, long EDM build, muddy strings and bass, imitation of a named artist
```
