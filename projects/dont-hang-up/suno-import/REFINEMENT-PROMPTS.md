# Suno 定向修订提示

这些文字用于已有结果的对话式修订，或改写后再生成。它们描述期望，不保证精确保留未改部分。每轮先选一个主要问题。

## Hook 不够明确
Keep the lyrics and late-night melodic-trap mood. Make the chorus title a compact, repeatable sung motif with three clear accents on DON'T, HANG, UP. Lengthen the vowel in UP, then leave a short rest. Simplify surrounding runs so the title is easy to sing back. Preserve the contrast between rhythmic verses and a more open sung chorus.

## Verse 太像朗读
Perform both verses as connected melodic rap in natural American English. Use syncopated entrances, weak unstressed words, and clear stressed rhyme vowels. Keep most verse notes within a narrow range, with small rises at emotional turns. Let phrases flow across beats and leave breaths at the ends of four-line groups.

## 女声接管主唱
Keep the male singer as the main narrator throughout both verses and all choruses. Use the airy female voice only for the original refrain in the intro and bridge, brief responses between male phrases, and subtle final-chorus harmonies. Give the two voices separate space.

## Beat 太满
Keep the vocal performance prominent. Thin the arrangement to soft keys, a restrained plucked motif, a warm tuned 808 and sparse half-time drums. Remove melodic fills while important verse lines are sung. Put short hat rolls and delay throws in the gaps after phrases.

## 结尾没有情绪变化
Preserve the familiar chorus motif in the final chorus, but clearly sing I WON'T HANG UP instead of DON'T HANG UP. The singer now offers the care he previously needed. Make the delivery warmer and more assured, with light sung doubles and a restrained female harmony.

## 使用本项目的乐器旋律导唱作音频参考
Use this original guide as a reference for the hook rhythm, melodic contour, half-time pocket and section contrast. The electric-piano line represents the intended male topline; the higher bell line represents the female refrain. Reinterpret those guide lines as expressive sung voices using the supplied lyrics. Keep a male lead and a female supporting refrain. Build a natural vocal performance with connected syllables and breathing space; the guide instrument timbres are placeholders.
