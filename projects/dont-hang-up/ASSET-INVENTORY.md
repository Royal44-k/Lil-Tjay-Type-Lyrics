The three release packages are kept for convenience. They repeat material already visible as individual files: `DONT-HANG-UP-2026-09-17.zip` is the initial handoff; `DONT-HANG-UP-SUNO-IMPORT.zip` includes the six prompt files and hook preview WAV; `DONT-HANG-UP-v2-PRODUCTION-PACK.zip` includes the v2 production files plus copies of two research documents. Those two research documents are also available in `v1/`.

The three WAVs under `v2/` are distinct outputs: a full instrumental, a full instrumental-timbre melody guide, and a short hook preview. None contains a sung vocal. The v1 MIDI and v2 MIDI are different stages of the composition. The v2 JSON named `source-v1-composition.json` is retained because `v2/build_demo.py` uses it as its input.

No third-party song recording, music video, extracted commercial sample, Halsey or Lil Tjay vocal, or completed Suno take is included. Links in research notes identify sources; they are not archived copies of those sources.
