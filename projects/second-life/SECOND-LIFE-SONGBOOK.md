# SECOND LIFE — 完整歌词册

原创英文歌词，2026-09-18。方括号为演唱提示。制作细节见 ALBUM-BLUEPRINT.md。


## 01 NO RECEIPT

```text
[Intro: chopped vocal, filtered guitar]
Still... still...
Cut the tag.

[Chorus: clipped melodic chant]
No receipt, no return.
Can't buy back what I had to learn.
Cut the tag, let it burn.
I'm still me when the numbers turn.

[Verse 1: dry rap, measured delivery]
Mom stitched the cuff where the thread went thin,
Said, "It ain't worn out if you're still in it."
I wore that coat to a room full of grins,
They loved the rough edges, then asked me to trim 'em.
"Give us the hunger, but clean up the kitchen."
Hard to sell scars when they show how you got 'em.
They wanted the climb with the stairs gone missing,
Wanted my name in a box at the bottom.
I watched them turn "we" into one man's vision,
Heard my whole block called a niche demographic.
So when that gold pen slid into position,
I asked who gets paid when my pain goes platinum.

[Chorus: full drums, gang responses]
No receipt, no return.
Can't buy back what I had to learn.
Cut the tag, let it burn.
I'm still me when the numbers turn.

[Verse 2: lower register, growing intensity]
First big check, I sent Mom the balance,
Then sat in the car with the engine dead.
I thought relief would arrive like a package;
It came as a night she could sleep instead.
My boy wants a cut; he was there for the basement,
His verse got erased when the deal got revised.
If I call that love, then I gotta arrange it
So his name and his rent both survive.
I'm learning the difference: a stage and a platform,
A hand in the picture, a hand on the load.
If I put my people in all of my captions,
Why's only my name on the paper I own?

[Breakdown: voice and kick, stop before the return]
Hold that flash.
Bring the credits back.
You can own this record.
You don't own my past.

[Final Chorus: wide doubles, restrained lead]
No receipt, no return.
Can't buy back what I had to learn.
Cut the tag, let it burn.
I'm still me when the numbers turn.
No receipt, no return.
Put their names on what we earned.
Cut the tag, let it burn.
I'm still me when the numbers turn.

[Outro: chopped vocal, hard cut]
Still... still...
No return.
```


## 02 FAST FORWARD

```text
[Intro: granular vocal stutter]
Fast, fast...
Wait.

[Chorus: bright melody, abrupt rests]
Fast-forward, fast-forward, wait, wait, wait.
I got to tomorrow, but I missed today.
Fast-forward, fast-forward, wait, wait, wait.
Everybody loves me when I run this pace.

[Verse 1: nimble melodic rap]
Clock on the dash says I made good time,
Cold fries in the bag say I missed that dinner.
Three unread texts with a "you outside?"
I type "on my way" like a prayer for a sinner.
Appointment moved, but the rent stayed rigid,
Calendar full, every square got thinner.
I can put my whole life up in a minute,
Still can't find one for the people in it.
Thumb on the screen like I'm checking a pulse,
New little number, another small jolt.
I say it's a grind, they call it results;
Nobody counts what I lose in the totals.

[Pre-Chorus: drums falling away]
If I slow down, do I still get a place?
Watch the whole room freeze when I say...

[Chorus: bright melody, abrupt rests]
Fast-forward, fast-forward, wait, wait, wait.
I got to tomorrow, but I missed today.
Fast-forward, fast-forward, wait, wait, wait.
Everybody loves me when I run this pace.

[Verse 2: conversational, fewer doubles]
Train door closes; I catch my reflection,
Face stretched out in the tunnel's blue light.
I know every trick for improving connection,
Still let my brother ring out twice.
He sent me a clip of his kid at the school play,
I watched it on mute with a meeting on deck.
The crowd in that gym doesn't care what the views say;
She waves at the back where I said I would sit.
No tragic speech, no deleting my contacts;
I traded one shift for a seat on their floor.
Still had a bill on the fridge when I got back,
But she'd yelled, "You're here!" from halfway through the door.

[Bridge: empty downbeat, stop-start percussion]
Wait.
Let the next train go.
Wait.
I can take the long way home.

[Final Chorus: open melody, fewer glitches]
Fast-forward, fast-forward, wait, wait, wait.
I got to tomorrow, but I missed today.
Fast-forward, fast-forward, wait, wait, wait.
Let the world run on; I can walk my pace.
Fast-forward, fast-forward, wait, wait, wait.
Leave a little room for a day with no race.
Fast-forward, fast-forward, wait, wait, wait.
I can lose a little speed and still find my way.

[Outro: dry voice, tail stretched into room noise]
Wait.
I'm here now.
```


## 03 ROOM TONE

```text
[Intro: intimate low female voice, room ambience]
You can put your things down.
It's been a long day.

[Chorus: soft, memorable descending melody]
Stay for the quiet, quiet.
You don't have to fill the air.
We don't need to call it forever
For tonight to mean we care.

[Verse 1: conversational singing rap]
Your jacket on the chair, rain in the seams,
Two bowls in the sink, one chipped at the rim.
You ask if I'm mad; I say, "Just a week
Of making myself small enough to fit in."
Then I make a joke, do the voice, get a laugh,
Old habit: be easy, be worth having back.
You slide me the plate and you don't take the bait,
Let the punchline fall flat, let me finish the day.
I learned how to charm when I needed a ride,
Got good at "I'm good" with a knot in my side.
You don't pull it out like a thread from my sleeve;
Just ask if I'd like you to stay while I eat.

[Pre-Chorus: held harmony, close microphone]
If I don't tell the story, will you sit through the space?
Your knee brushes mine; I don't turn away.

[Chorus: soft doubles, warm bass]
Stay for the quiet, quiet.
You don't have to fill the air.
We don't need to call it forever
For tonight to mean we care.

[Verse 2: intimate, steady, unhurried]
You say, "I can't always know what you need."
I want to get sharp, but I let myself breathe.
"Then ask me," I say, "and I'll try to be clear.
Some nights I need company, some nights it's sleep."
We talk about money, the leak in the ceiling,
The plant by the window we both keep forgetting.
No big revelation; you rinse off the dishes.
I dry them beside you, the music still skipping.
You head out at ten, like you said that you would.
I lock up behind you; the quiet stays good.
It used to feel empty the second you'd leave;
Now some of the room finally belongs to me.

[Bridge: nearly a cappella]
Same four walls.
I can hear myself in here.

[Final Chorus: tender, settled]
Stay for the quiet, quiet.
You don't have to fill the air.
You can leave and I can miss you
Without disappearing here.

[Outro: original female sample, natural voice]
There's still life in me.
```


## 04 SECOND LIFE

```text
[Intro: exposed original female vocal]
There's still life in me.

[Chorus: warm male melody, female answer]
Second life, same heartbeat.
Scuffed-up shoes, still miles in me.
Second life, save a seat.
Not brand new, there's still life in me.

[Verse 1: vivid storytelling, relaxed pocket]
Shop downstairs had a speaker for free,
Sat by the door with a cord worn thin.
Man at the counter said, "Take it and see."
We stripped that wire, let the low end in.
He let me make noise till the shutters came down;
I looped that first hum till it sounded like home.
A kid from upstairs kept tapping the counter;
We gave him the mic; he forgot to let go.
He sang out of time, then he laughed when he heard it;
I moved the whole drum break to follow his swing.
I used to erase every take that wasn't perfect;
That was the one everybody kept singing.

[Chorus: melodic rap, bright backing responses]
Second life, same heartbeat.
Scuffed-up shoes, still miles in me.
Second life, save a seat.
Not brand new, there's still life in me.

[Verse 2: restrained emotion, bass and piano]
Rent went up twice; he couldn't renew it.
A broker took photos before we were through.
We borrowed a van; six of us had to move it.
The kid held the mic the whole ride to the room.
We didn't get saved by a song or a slogan;
We took extra shifts, found a room we could split.
The speaker still rattles; we leave that part open,
A little reminder of who carried it.
If I get a stage, let the names fill the back wall;
If I get a door, put a hold on the latch.
There's a kid in the hall with a demo that's half done;
Run it again. Let me hear what you have.

[Bridge: breakbeat, strings answering the voice]
That's the hum from the floor.
That's the knock at the door.
Keep the laugh before the take.
Let it play. Let it play.

[Final Chorus: layered voices, open and celebratory]
Second life, same heartbeat.
Scuffed-up shoes, still miles in me.
Second life, save a seat.
Not brand new, there's still life in me.
Second life, same heartbeat.
Leave a little space beside the beat.
Second life, save a seat.
There's still life in me.

[Outro: female voice alone, leave the breath]
There's still life in me.
```
