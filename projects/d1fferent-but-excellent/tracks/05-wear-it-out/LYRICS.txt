[Intro - quiet male voice]
Cold came early.
I found it upstairs.

[Chorus - aching but steady]
Wear it out, wear it out.
You'd hate to see it hanging in an empty house.
Take it downtown, let it catch some rain.
I can keep you close and let the color change.

[Verse 1 - close, almost spoken]
Your sister said, "Take it," the week of the service,
I held it like something too good for my hands.
One little burn where you leaned on the furnace,
Two bus tickets and a peppermint jammed
Deep in the pocket you never would empty.
Said it was luck. We both knew it was trash.
I laugh for a second, then feel like I'm cheating;
Grief got a way of inspecting a laugh.
Kept it upstairs through the spring and the summer,
Walked past the hook like it wasn't all mine.
Weather got mean and I reached for another,
Then heard how you'd clown me for freezing outside.

[Chorus - aching but steady]
Wear it out, wear it out.
You'd hate to see it hanging in an empty house.
Take it downtown, let it catch some rain.
I can keep you close and let the color change.

[Verse 2 - warmer, more rhythmic]
Wore it for coffee, got milk on the collar,
Stood by the sink with my throat getting tight.
Called up your sister, still running the water:
"He spilled on that thing nearly every night."
Took it back out with the stain still a little there,
Wind found the tear that you'd patched from inside.
Girl at the counter said, "Cool, is it vintage?"
I said, "It's my friend's," and I didn't say "was."
Maybe next winter I'll need a new lining,
Maybe one day it'll be past repair.
I don't have to keep every thread from unwinding
To know who was with me when nobody cared.

[Bridge - piano and voice]
Some days I leave it.
Some days I don't.
You're not less with me
When I leave it home.

[Final Chorus - harmony enters late]
Wear it out, wear it out.
You'd hate to see it hanging in an empty house.
Take it downtown, let it catch some rain.
I can keep you close and let the color change.

[Outro]
First cold morning.
I put it on.
