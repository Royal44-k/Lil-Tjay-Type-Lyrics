## DBE01 — ONE OF ONE

```text
[Intro - close male voice]
They said, "Pick one."
I kept both.

[Chorus - melodic, confident]
One of one, one of one.
They can't put a number on what I've become.
One of one, let it run.
I was doing too much; now it ain't enough.

[Verse 1 - conversational, building]
Wrote on the back of the forms they were grading,
Drummed on the desk till they moved me outside.
Coach said, "You're quick, but you keep hesitating."
I heard a whole other count in my mind.
Mom took the sleeves up; the cuffs still were crooked.
I wore that jacket like somebody chose it.
Kid with the clean ones would laugh when I stood up;
Same kid asks me what store I got those in.
Didn't get good from a room full of doubters,
Got good when nobody stayed for the hours.
Took what was off in the take, made it louder,
Left in the breath where I ran out of power.

[Pre-Chorus - half sung]
They want a name for the way that I do it.
Give me the room and I'll walk you right through it.

[Chorus - melodic, confident]
One of one, one of one.
They can't put a number on what I've become.
One of one, let it run.
I was doing too much; now it ain't enough.

[Verse 2 - assertive, varied pocket]
First little check, I got tires for the winter,
Kept one receipt in the case with the strings.
They saw the chain in a cropped little picture;
Didn't see brake pads and boring grown things.
Now every cousin got bets on the outcome,
Tell me I'm next like the date's getting near.
Love how they love me; get tired of the countdown.
What if I'm good and it still takes me years?
What if the trick is to stay with the strange part,
Keep that bent note when the clean one would sell?
I want the lights. I can say that with my chest out.
I want a voice I can live with as well.

[Bridge - low, drums reduced]
Call me the one.
I still gotta choose.
Put it all on me.
I still need my room.

[Final Chorus - layered but clear]
One of one, one of one.
They can't put a number on what I've become.
One of one, let it run.
I was doing too much; now it ain't enough.
One of one, one of one.
Let the strange little part of me turn all the way up.
One of one, let it run.
I got more in the room than the world heard once.

[Outro]
Keep that part.
The part that bends.
```


## DBE02 — AFTER THE FLASH

```text
[Intro - dry, quiet]
Elevator mirror.
Same face.

[Chorus - intimate melodic rap]
After the flash, after the flash,
Who am I when the room goes black?
All of that light, none of it lasts.
You know my name; do you know where I'm at?

[Verse 1 - low, behind the beat]
Key won't read, got my hands full of flowers,
Three missed dinners and a shirt full of heat.
Everybody said, "Man, the city's yours now."
I can't find a place that'll let me just eat.
Scroll through the shots till I look like I felt it,
Pick the one smiling, then crop out the wristband.
That little plastic was cutting my skin up;
Funny what stays when you clean up the image.
Phone on the pillow, still bright in the dark,
Read every "proud of you" twice before dawn.
One from my brother says, "Look at that haircut."
I hear how he'd say it and finally yawn.

[Chorus - restrained, wider harmony]
After the flash, after the flash,
Who am I when the room goes black?
All of that light, none of it lasts.
You know my name; do you know where I'm at?

[Verse 2 - increasingly direct]
Breakfast at noon with my friend from the old job,
He tells me the freezer broke down on his shift.
I start on the party; he asks if I slept yet.
I laugh at how fast he gets under the script.
He doesn't ask me for names or a favor,
Just says, "You been checking that screen for a minute."
I put it face down by the salt and the paper;
He gives me the whole other half of his biscuit.
Maybe I don't need the night to mean nothing
Just 'cause the morning came quieter than planned.
I liked being seen. I can say that in public.
I like being known by a few where I stand.

[Bridge - almost spoken]
One more picture?
Give me a sec.
I want to be here
Before I forget.

[Final Chorus - warmer, no large build]
After the flash, after the flash,
Who am I when the room goes black?
All of that light, none of it lasts.
Sit for a minute; I'll tell you where I'm at.

[Outro]
Leave the phone there.
Finish your food.
```

## DBE04 — SIDE DOOR

```text
[Intro - clipped male voice]
Same number.
Different rules.

[Chorus - low chant with melodic tail]
Side door, side door.
Knock first, like you did before.
Same love, new lock.
We can still be close with the cameras off.

[Verse 1 - precise, tense]
Got to the room and my cousin was filming,
Panned from the shoes to the view through the glass.
Didn't ask once before showing the building,
Street on the sign and the room where I crash.
I took down the story; he said I was changing.
I said, "Yeah, I got somewhere worth keeping."
He heard a rich kid embarrassed by family.
I heard the hallway at two while I'm sleeping.
Both of us talking like somebody's watching,
Both of us reaching for something to win.
He used to save me the end of his sandwich;
I don't want strangers deciding on him.

[Chorus - low chant with melodic tail]
Side door, side door.
Knock first, like you did before.
Same love, new lock.
We can still be close with the cameras off.

[Verse 2 - tighter rhythm, controlled anger]
Called him on Tuesday, no audience with us,
Said what I meant without making it vicious.
You can come over; the camera stays off.
Ask 'fore you bring someone, even your plus-one.
He said, "You talk like I'm one of them vultures."
I said, "I'm talking 'cause you ain't a stranger."
Some things are private and some things are over;
These ain't the same, but they both need an answer.
We didn't hug with a score in the background.
He took a breath, said, "All right," then we ate.
Next time he knocked, put his phone in his jacket.
I slid him a plate. Didn't call it a change.

[Bridge - bare voice, long rests]
I can love you.
I can lock this.
Same hand on the handle.
Both things honest.

[Final Chorus - more open, still restrained]
Side door, side door.
Knock first, like you did before.
Same love, new lock.
We can still be close with the cameras off.

[Outro]
Knock first.
I might be home.
```


## DBE05 — WEAR IT OUT

```text
[Intro - quiet male voice]
Cold came early.
I found it upstairs.

[Chorus - aching but steady]
Wear it out, wear it out.
You'd hate to see it hanging in an empty house.
Take it downtown, let it catch some rain.
I can keep you close and let the color change.

[Verse 1 - close, almost spoken]
Your sister said, "Take it," the week of the service,
I held it like something too good for my hands.
One little burn where you leaned on the furnace,
Two bus tickets and a peppermint jammed
Deep in the pocket you never would empty.
Said it was luck. We both knew it was trash.
I laugh for a second, then feel like I'm cheating;
Grief got a way of inspecting a laugh.
Kept it upstairs through the spring and the summer,
Walked past the hook like it wasn't all mine.
Weather got mean and I reached for another,
Then heard how you'd clown me for freezing outside.

[Chorus - aching but steady]
Wear it out, wear it out.
You'd hate to see it hanging in an empty house.
Take it downtown, let it catch some rain.
I can keep you close and let the color change.

[Verse 2 - warmer, more rhythmic]
Wore it for coffee, got milk on the collar,
Stood by the sink with my throat getting tight.
Called up your sister, still running the water:
"He spilled on that thing nearly every night."
Took it back out with the stain still a little there,
Wind found the tear that you'd patched from inside.
Girl at the counter said, "Cool, is it vintage?"
I said, "It's my friend's," and I didn't say "was."
Maybe next winter I'll need a new lining,
Maybe one day it'll be past repair.
I don't have to keep every thread from unwinding
To know who was with me when nobody cared.

[Bridge - piano and voice]
Some days I leave it.
Some days I don't.
You're not less with me
When I leave it home.

[Final Chorus - harmony enters late]
Wear it out, wear it out.
You'd hate to see it hanging in an empty house.
Take it downtown, let it catch some rain.
I can keep you close and let the color change.

[Outro]
First cold morning.
I put it on.
```

## DBE07 — D1FFERENT BUT EXCELLENT

```text
[Intro - male, clear diction]
Different.
Yeah, I kept that word.

[Chorus - spacious melodic rap]
Different, but excellent.
I can put my whole chest in it.
One of one, still working on it.
I know what I got; I ain't finished with it.

[Verse 1 - measured, unsentimental]
School report folded behind the old license,
Teacher wrote "different" in blue by my name.
Mom added "excellent," pressed through the paper,
Said, "They can both fit." I kept it that way.
I took it to mean I could never be average,
Turned every Tuesday to opening night.
Played them the good ones and hid all the practice,
Started to hate what I hadn't got right.
Then one little show with the room half empty,
One kid up front knew a line that I cut.
I said, "That's an old one." He said, "It still gets me."
I stopped trying to tell him it wasn't enough.

[Chorus - spacious melodic rap]
Different, but excellent.
I can put my whole chest in it.
One of one, still working on it.
I know what I got; I ain't finished with it.

[Verse 2 - warmer, steady momentum]
Back in the room with a hum in the cable,
No little crowd saying, "This is the one."
I move the kick back, make space on the table,
Listen to what I kept trying to outrun.
There's a thin note where my breath didn't make it;
This time I sing it in reach of my voice.
Some days you keep it and some days replace it.
Knowing the difference is part of the choice.
I want the big stage, the checks and the freedom.
Still want to sleep when the night's finally done.
If I'm the chosen one, let me keep choosing
Who gets my time and what work gets my love.

[Bridge - exposed voice then quiet harmony]
One doesn't have to mean alone.
I can bring the people who knew me home.
Leave the take with the question in it.
Play it back. Let me sit with it.

[Final Chorus - open, communal backing]
Different, but excellent.
I can put my whole chest in it.
One of one, still working on it.
I know what I got; I ain't finished with it.
Different, but excellent.
I can put my whole chest in it.
One of one, still working on it.
Tomorrow I'll get up and get back in it.

[Outro - male, quiet]
Save that version.
I'll be here tomorrow.
```
