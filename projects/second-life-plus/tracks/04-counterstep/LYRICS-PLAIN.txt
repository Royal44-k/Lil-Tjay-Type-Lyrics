Sole came loose at the edge of the circle,
One cheap shoe with the tongue hanging out.
Boy by the booth did a slow little smirk;
I felt my whole face get hot in the crowd.
Used to rehearse how I'd look being fearless,
Down to the shrug and the tilt of my chin.
Then I caught the wrong count, everybody could hear it;
Funny, the floor didn't swallow me in.
So I bent that knee, let the wrong thing travel,
Made a small turn out of almost a fall.
Now the kid with the camera is losing his angle;
He can't hold the frame with us filling it all.

Girl in the silver says, "Run that again."
I say, "Half of it happened; the rest was pretend."
She adds a quick shoulder, I borrow the bend,
Now nobody knows where the old pattern ends.
Suit by the bar with his tie pulled neat,
He puts down the glass when he catches the beat.
Shoes worth a paycheck still step on my feet;
We both crack up. Let the drummer repeat.
I spent too much time being hard to embarrass,
Guarding a face that nobody could touch.
Tonight I look foolish, and nobody cares;
Somebody laughs and we all loosen up.
