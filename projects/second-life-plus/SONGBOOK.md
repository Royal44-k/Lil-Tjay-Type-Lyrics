## SLP02 — IVORY HOURS

```text
[Intro]

[Chorus]
Ivory hours, nobody claps.
I let the last note find its way back.
You never said sorry; I still hear you laugh.
Both things can fit in the song that I have.

[Verse 1 - close, conversational]
Back from the service, I sit at your keys,
Cup-ring moon on the lid by the lamp.
I could sell it for less than the movers would need,
But I can't lift the phone with that number in hand.
You taught me the left hand and hated my timing,
Tapped on the wood till I swallowed the tune.
I learned to play softly to keep you from sighing;
Now I hit that wrong note, let it stay in the room.
Mom says you mellowed. My brother says hardly.
We carry a different old man in our heads.
He fixed every hinge, never said he was sorry;
I'm sorting the screws from the things left unsaid.

[Chorus]
Ivory hours, nobody claps.
I let the last note find its way back.
You never said sorry; I still hear you laugh.
Both things can fit in the song that I have.

[Verse 2 - low, reflective]
Found a small tape with a date on the label,
You counting me in, then forgetting the count.
I laugh at the part where you knock on the table;
You laugh right behind me. I hadn't heard that sound.
For years I had filed you in one little folder:
The voice in the hall when the bottle got low.
But this doesn't cancel the nights I got older
Before I was ready, or make them less so.
I keep both the takes when I cut up the sample,
The soft little hum and the crack in the chair.
Forgiveness is still not a word I can manage;
Tonight I just leave a small gap in the snare.

[Bridge - almost spoken]
I don't have to make you good
To miss you when the house gets still.

[Final Chorus]
Ivory hours, nobody claps.
I let the last note find its way back.
You never said sorry; I still hear you laugh.
I leave a little air for the parts I don't have.

[Outro]
One note hanging.
I let it hang.
```

## SLP04 — COUNTERSTEP

```text
[Intro]
One, two, three...
Come in.

[Chorus]
Wrong foot, right time.
Let the whole room catch this slide.
Counterstep, switch sides.
If you came to watch, you can get inside.

[Verse 1 - playful, percussive]
Sole came loose at the edge of the circle,
One cheap shoe with the tongue hanging out.
Boy by the booth did a slow little smirk;
I felt my whole face get hot in the crowd.
Used to rehearse how I'd look being fearless,
Down to the shrug and the tilt of my chin.
Then I caught the wrong count, everybody could hear it;
Funny, the floor didn't swallow me in.
So I bent that knee, let the wrong thing travel,
Made a small turn out of almost a fall.
Now the kid with the camera is losing his angle;
He can't hold the frame with us filling it all.

[Chorus]
Wrong foot, right time.
Let the whole room catch this slide.
Counterstep, switch sides.
If you came to watch, you can get inside.

[Verse 2 - looser, smiling]
Girl in the silver says, "Run that again."
I say, "Half of it happened; the rest was pretend."
She adds a quick shoulder, I borrow the bend,
Now nobody knows where the old pattern ends.
Suit by the bar with his tie pulled neat,
He puts down the glass when he catches the beat.
Shoes worth a paycheck still step on my feet;
We both crack up. Let the drummer repeat.
I spent too much time being hard to embarrass,
Guarding a face that nobody could touch.
Tonight I look foolish, and nobody cares;
Somebody laughs and we all loosen up.

[Bridge - violin exposed]
One, two, three...
Four gets in for free.
One, two, three...
Leave that count to me.

[Final Chorus]
Wrong foot, right time.
Let the whole room catch this slide.
Counterstep, switch sides.
If you came to watch, you can get inside.
Wrong foot, right time.
Let the whole room catch this slide.
Counterstep, switch sides.
Whole room moving, nobody on the side.

[Outro]
Counterstep.
Run that again.
```

## SLP07 — LAST TRAIN, FIRST LIGHT

```text
[Intro]

[Chorus]

I can love this place and leave tonight.
Last train, first light.
There's a life I haven't met on the other side.

[Verse 1 - reflective melodic rap]
Mom folds a sandwich in foil for the journey,
Asks if the place has a stove and a lock.
I say, "Yeah," while my brother keeps turning
The wheel on my suitcase that always gets caught.
Dad writes the name of a guy he once worked with,
Slips it inside with a twenty and change.
Says, "Call if you need him." I know what the words miss;
He spent the whole week trying out "Be safe."
Some of my friends make a joke out of leaving,
As if what I want makes the old place worth less.
I love where I'm from; I don't owe it my breathing.
One walks me down, says, "Go give it your best."

One more wave till the platform gets small.
I can go without undoing it all.

[Chorus]
Last train, first light.
I can love this place and leave tonight.

[Verse 2 - conversational, opening up]

Where I call home; I say, "Give me a week."
A room with one chair and a crack in the plaster,
Nothing like all of the pictures I sent.

Send it to Mom; she says, "Now pay your rent."

Last train, first light.

First light.
```

## SLP08 — SECOND LIFE

```text
[Intro: exposed original female vocal]
There's still life in me.

[Chorus: warm male melody, female answer]
Second life, same heartbeat.
Scuffed-up shoes, still miles in me.
Second life, save a seat.
Not brand new, there's still life in me.

[Verse 1: vivid storytelling, relaxed pocket]
Shop downstairs had a speaker for free,
Sat by the door with a cord worn thin.
Man at the counter said, "Take it and see."
We stripped that wire, let the low end in.
He let me make noise till the shutters came down;
I looped that first hum till it sounded like home.
A kid from upstairs kept tapping the counter;
We gave him the mic; he forgot to let go.
He sang out of time, then he laughed when he heard it;
I moved the whole drum break to follow his swing.
I used to erase every take that wasn't perfect;
That was the one everybody kept singing.

[Chorus: melodic rap, bright backing responses]
Second life, same heartbeat.
Scuffed-up shoes, still miles in me.
Second life, save a seat.
Not brand new, there's still life in me.

[Verse 2: restrained emotion, bass and piano]
Rent went up twice; he couldn't renew it.
A broker took photos before we were through.
We borrowed a van; six of us had to move it.
The kid held the mic the whole ride to the room.
We didn't get saved by a song or a slogan;
We took extra shifts, found a room we could split.
The speaker still rattles; we leave that part open,
A little reminder of who carried it.
If I get a stage, let the names fill the back wall;
If I get a door, put a hold on the latch.
There's a kid in the hall with a demo that's half done;
Run it again. Let me hear what you have.

[Bridge: breakbeat, strings answering the voice]
That's the hum from the floor.
That's the knock at the door.
Keep the laugh before the take.
Let it play. Let it play.

[Final Chorus: layered voices, open and celebratory]
Second life, same heartbeat.
Scuffed-up shoes, still miles in me.
Second life, save a seat.
Not brand new, there's still life in me.
Second life, same heartbeat.
Leave a little space beside the beat.
Second life, save a seat.
There's still life in me.

[Outro: female voice alone, leave the breath]
There's still life in me.
```
