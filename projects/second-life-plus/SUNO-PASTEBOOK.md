## SLP02 — IVORY HOURS

### Title

```text
IVORY HOURS
```

### Style

```text
Low male voice, close conversational rap with a small restrained sung refrain. English underground hip-hop and jazz rap, 88 BPM, D minor. An original upright-piano performance resampled into uneven two-beat fragments; audible felt and damper detail, warm bass, dry kick and rimshot, light swing. Reflective family storytelling: grief and affection coexist with anger. Verses use rhythmic speech, one line per bar; chorus uses longer notes and breathing space. Expose the piano in the bridge. Honest and intimate, clear consonants, natural pauses, no grand emotional climax.
```

### Exclude

```text
power-ballad belting, dense hi-hat rolls, stadium choir, heavy vinyl noise
```

## SLP04 — COUNTERSTEP


### Style

```text
Playful male rap, sharp consonants and a compact singable chant. English orchestral drill, 144 BPM, G minor. Build the beat around an original short violin waltz phrase: retain its lilting three-part motion over a firm four-beat drill grid. Dry spiccato bow attacks, a few pizzicato replies, sparse 808 glides and syncopated kick. The hook is Wrong foot, right time; confident and welcoming, not menacing. Verses tell a dance-floor story of turning a stumble into a move. Brief exposed violin bridge, then a punchy final hook. Two fast bars per verse line; leave room around punchlines.
```

```text

```

```text
English melodic rap and soulful sample-based trap, with a short self-played breakbeat passage and chamber strings. 144 BPM, A major, warm half-time pocket. Grounded male lead, easy melodic title hook, distinct warm female answers. Open with an original female phrase, "There's still life in me"; let that voice answer the male hook. Scuffed piano sample, rounded 808, dry claps, a small human-sounding drum break, cello pulse and high string replies. Narrative verses about repairing a speaker, losing a neighborhood shop and building a shared music room. Each verse line spans two fast bars. Bridge opens into breakbeat motion without changing tempo. Final chorus brings earlier vocal fragments together, leaves room for the words and feels communal rather than grandiose. Last four bars expose the female source phrase and its breath. Hope with weight, tactile detail, strong bass clarity, no triumphalist wall of sound.
```
