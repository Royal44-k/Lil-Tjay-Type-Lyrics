# American melodic trap: production and lyric craft research

Research date: 2026-09-11. Read material is distinguished from recording analysis; no claim here depends on having listened to the source recordings.

## Primary and specialist sources

1. [DJ Toomp, Red Bull Music Academy lecture, 2008](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/dj-toomp-there-it-is/). Toomp describes Atlanta trap's street context, heavy low-end and slow riding feel; he also describes his own keyboard/orchestral approach and emphasizes T.I.'s distinctive delivery. Use the combination of a specific voice and a beat that supports it, rather than treating instrumentation alone as a genre guarantee.

2. [Metro Boomin, Sonny Digital and Zaytoven, RBMA roundtable, 2016](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/metro-boomin-sonny-digital-zaytoven/). Zaytoven discusses church organ playing; Sonny describes assembling songs from vocal takes using a producer's approach. These firsthand accounts support treating melody, rhythmic phrasing and arrangement as interdependent crafts. The panel's cultural context is summarized separately in the culture notes.

3. [Roland: Roland and the sound of Hip-Hop](https://www.roland.com/uk/groovebox/hiphop/?lang=en-GB). The manufacturer's commissioned history traces drum machines, sampling and circulation of sounds across scenes. The TR-808's history is wider than trap. Avoid using "808" as a synonym for the whole culture.

4. [Hudson Music: Trap Style Drumming sample](https://hudsonmusic.com/wp-content/uploads/trapstyledrumming-sample.pdf). The drumming text describes a common half-time bounce and combinations of duple/triple-rate hi-hat figures. These are characteristic tools, not requirements for every track, and the book discusses multiple rhythmic forms.

5. [iZotope: How to Mix 808s for a Powerful Low End, 2023](https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/how-to-mix-808s). Distinguishes modern bass-line use of the term "808" from its drum-machine origin and discusses its relationship with the kick. Applied decision: give the demo an audible low-frequency line and some saturation, while checking PCM headroom.

6. [iZotope: How to Mix Hi-Hats](https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/how-to-mix-hi-hats). The indexed excerpt recommends velocity, timing and duration variation in programmed patterns. The full-page read timed out; only that accessible excerpt is used. Applied decision: short fills and varying hat dynamics, with space preserved around the hook.

7. [XXL: The Break Presents Lil TJay, January 9, 2019](https://www.xxlmag.com/lil-tjay-interview-the-break/). His own answers name both rap and pop/R&B listening influences, and emphasize work ethic, talent and hunger. This supports understanding his melodic delivery alongside ambition and rap identity. It does not define all New York music or all melodic rap.

8. [Berklee: Natural Music in Language](https://www.berklee.edu/berklee-now/news/berklee-riffs-natural-music-in-language). Applied decision: English syllable stress governs the new hook. Short function words link emphasized words; sustained notes are placed on meaningful syllables.

## Practical choices for STILL GOT ME

These are original composition decisions, not measurements or transcriptions of Born 2 Be Great:

- A 144 BPM project with a 72 BPM half-time feel and 16 fast-grid bars per hook.
- F-sharp minor with a light/dark harmonic contrast, a short plucked motif, sparse keys, a saturated 808-style bass and syncopated kick.
- A sung title motif with rests, shorter rhythmic connecting words, and held /i:/ endings.
- Verses retain ambition, social detail and self-contradiction: a parent's bill, a status purchase, a friend's record deal, unspoken needs, and family obligations.
- The fictional narrator's voice stays consistent. No regional biography, gang membership or real-world crime is assigned to the user or to a named artist.
- The rendered WAV is an instrumental topline sketch. It cannot demonstrate the eventual vocalist's accent, timbre, consonants, pitch correction or performance chemistry with the beat; those remain recording decisions.
